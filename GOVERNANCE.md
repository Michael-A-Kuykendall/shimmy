# Shimmy governance

Shimmy is maintained by its original author, Michael A. Kuykendall.
It is open source, not open contribution. Co-maintainers must request approval
by email before submitting work; see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The maintainer controls architecture, licensing and publication decisions.
An approved private candidate is not approval for public source updates,
tags, registry uploads, hosted workflows or images. Release authorization
applies to exact reviewed artifacts and support claims.

Issues and respectful discussion are welcome. Report vulnerabilities privately
under [SECURITY](SECURITY.md). Component/model licenses remain applicable.
